🥔 Creamy Potato & Cheese Bake

A rich, golden-crusted potato gratin with layers of sharp cheddar, cream, and fresh herbs — comfort food elevated.

⏱ Prep: 20 min
🔥 Cook: 55 min
🍽 Serves: 4
📊 Medium Difficulty
🌾 Vegetarian
Ingredients
  • 800g Yukon Gold potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 200g sharp cheddar cheese, grated
  • 300ml heavy cream
  • 100ml whole milk
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 50g parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • Salt & black pepper to taste
  • Fresh chives for garnish
Substitutions

↔Heavy cream → Coconut cream (dairy-free)

↔Cheddar → Gruyère or Gouda

↔Yukon Gold → Russet potatoes

↔Butter → Olive oil (vegan option)

Cooking Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Grease a 9×13 inch baking dish with butter.
  2. 2Warm cream, milk, garlic, and thyme in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming. Season generously with salt and pepper.
  3. 3Layer one-third of the potato slices overlapping in the dish. Add half the onions and one-third of the cheddar. Pour one-third of the cream mixture over.
  4. 4Repeat the layering process twice, finishing with potatoes on top. Pour remaining cream and top with remaining cheddar and all the parmesan.
  5. 5Dot the surface with small pieces of butter. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 35 minutes.
  6. 6Remove foil and bake uncovered for 20 more minutes until golden and bubbling. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.
  7. 7Garnish with fresh chives and cracked black pepper. Serve warm with a simple green salad.
